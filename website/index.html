Worship presentation for Windows

Lyrics, Scripture and the whole service, on screen and on time.

SanctuaryFlow helps church media teams plan the order of worship and present it on a projector or TV. It runs on one Windows computer and keeps working when the internet doesn’t.

Download for Windows See how it works

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit

Offline on Sunday

Songs, Bibles, services and media live on your computer. Nothing needs a connection while you present.

Two screens

You see the console with previews and what comes next. The room sees only the slides.

Made for the booth

Large controls, keyboard shortcuts, and a lock that keeps the screen steady while you prepare.

Your library, your computer

No account is needed. Backups go to a folder or USB drive you choose.

What it does

Everything Sunday needs, in one place.

01

Plan the service

Build the order of worship with songs, Scripture, announcements and videos. Bring sermon slides in from PowerPoint, a PDF or a Canva link. Reuse last week’s service as a template.

02

Songs that format themselves

Type or paste the lyrics once. Verses, choruses and bridges become clean slides: white letters on metallic gold, or any look you design. Fix a typo and the live screen updates straight away.

03

Scripture in seconds

Install a Bible translation once, then type a reference such as John 3:16. The whole chapter opens; choose one verse or several, and long verses are split into slides that stay readable.

04

Make it yours

Fonts, colours, pictures or video backgrounds, and transitions you can watch in the preview before you choose one. Set a look for songs, for Scripture and for announcements.

05

Light or dark

A bright interface for daytime and a dark one for dim sanctuaries, both in the same gold. Switch with one click at the top of every page.

Also included

  • Media Library with folders for pictures, videos and music.
  • Present now: any song or verse on screen without planning it first.
  • Black, clear and logo screens whenever you need a pause.
  • Backup and restore of the whole library, by hand or on a schedule.
  • A Home page with what’s coming up and the songs you use most.
  • Optional sync between your church’s computers.

Questions

Does it need the internet?

No. Presenting works offline. The internet is used only when you choose to: downloading a Bible translation, showing a Canva design, or syncing between computers.

Where are my songs and services kept?

In a library on your computer, inside your Windows user folder. Backup & Restore makes a complete copy you can keep on a USB drive or another disk.

Can I use a projector or a TV?

Yes. Connect it as a second display and SanctuaryFlow shows the output there in full screen, while your screen keeps the controls.

Which Bible translations can I use?

None are bundled. Inside the app you can download translations whose licence allows it from eBible.org, or import files you are permitted to use (USFM, USX, OSIS or CSV).

How do I update?

Download the newest installer from this page and run it. Your library is kept. It is still a good idea to make a backup first in Backup & Restore.

Why does Windows warn me about the installer?

SmartScreen warns about programs it has not seen often and that are not code-signed. Download only from this page, check the SHA-256 if you like, then choose More info and Run anyway.
